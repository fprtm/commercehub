# SIMULATED BUSINESS CASE — Project 1: WhatsApp Lead Capture & Auto-Responder

> This is a concept/simulated business case created to demonstrate a realistic client problem. "Rimba Apparel" is fictional. No real client, revenue, or testimonial is implied.

## Phase C — Business Simulation

**Business Type:** Small independent clothing/apparel seller, sells via Instagram content + WhatsApp for actual ordering.
**Company Size:** 2 people — the owner (does everything) + one part-time helper who packs orders.
**Industry:** Fashion/apparel retail, direct-to-consumer.
**Team Structure:** No dedicated sales or support staff. The owner personally replies to every WhatsApp message between other tasks.
**Current Workflow:** A customer sees a product on Instagram, DMs or WhatsApps to ask about size/price/availability. The owner replies manually, often hours later during a work break.
**Existing Pain Points:** Replies are slow, especially outside "shop hours." Some inquiries never get a reply at all because they get buried in the chat list. There is no record of who asked about what — if a customer doesn't follow up, the lead is simply gone.
**Trigger Event:** After a busy weekend post goes semi-viral, the owner realizes dozens of WhatsApp messages went unanswered for over a day, and at least a few customers said "nevermind" when finally answered — a visible, first-hand loss event (matches Phase 1 Pattern E: WhatsApp-as-storefront with no automation).
**Cost of the Problem (qualitative):** Lost sales from delayed/missed replies, no visibility into how many leads are actually coming in, and the owner's personal time consumed by message triage that could be partly automated.

## Phase D — Problem Before Solution

**Current State:** All customer contact happens in one personal WhatsApp number, checked manually and irregularly. Every reply is typed from scratch, even for the same recurring questions ("is this in stock," "how much is shipping").
**Problem Workflow:** Customer messages → message sits unread in a busy chat list → owner eventually sees it, possibly hours later → owner types a manual reply → if the owner is busy or the message gets buried, no reply is sent at all → no record of the interaction exists anywhere outside the chat thread itself.
**Root Cause:** The business has no system between "customer sends a WhatsApp message" and "someone follows up" — WhatsApp is being used purely as a raw chat tool, not as a lead-capture channel, and the business has scaled past what one person can reliably keep up with manually.
**Business Consequence:** If nothing changes, lead loss scales with growth — the busier the business gets (from its own marketing success), the worse the response time and the more leads silently disappear, directly undermining the value of any marketing spend that drives WhatsApp inquiries in the first place.
**Desired Future State:** Every WhatsApp message gets an instant acknowledgment even when the owner is unavailable, basic qualifying information (product interest, size) is captured automatically, and every inquiry is logged somewhere the owner can review — so no lead depends on the owner seeing a chat notification in time.
