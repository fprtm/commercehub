# Freelance Service Mapping — Project 1: WhatsApp Lead Capture & Auto-Responder

| Portfolio Capability | Client Problem | Freelance Service |
|---|---|---|
| Official WhatsApp Cloud API auto-responder | "I reply too slowly to WhatsApp inquiries and lose sales" | Entry Service: WhatsApp Auto-Responder & Lead Capture Setup |
| Two-question qualifying flow with retry-then-fallback | "I get a reply eventually but with no useful context to follow up" | Same Entry Service — the qualification step is what makes a captured lead actually useful, not just logged |
| Config-driven question script (no code change needed) | "I don't want to pay a developer every time I want to change my questions" | Differentiator inside the Entry Service pitch — supports the "documented, adaptable delivery" USP |
| Plain-language lead dashboard | "I have no visibility into how many inquiries I'm actually getting" | Bundled into the Entry Service; also the seed of the Core Service's dashboard (Project 2) |
| Failure logging (`FailedEvent`) instead of silent drops | "A freelancer built me something once and I never found out when it broke" | Direct proof for the Retainer Opportunity ("Monthly Systems Care & Monitoring") — this is the literal mechanism a retainer sells |
| Independent review catching real spec/code gaps before delivery | (Not a feature — a delivery-process signal) | Supports the Primary USP across all services: business-first discovery and a documented, verifiable delivery process, not "trust me it works" |
